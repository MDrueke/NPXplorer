
1. Long - term: Option to read Allen Mouse Atlas, let the user enter lambda-bregma distance (scale the atlas to it), and the insertion coordinates, and then label areas according to the atlas as an overlay over the plotted ephys data.

2. SpikeGLX-like electrode selection interface that is a popup that shows the probe (one or multiple shanks) and lets the user left click on channels to include them, right click to exclude them. Can also hold left click and drag to draw a rectangle that selects multiple electrodes.

3. Implement better ephys markers:
    a. Surface: Transition from low voltage fluctuations outside of the brain to high voltage amplitude fluctuations
    b. white matter, such as the corpus callosum (CC), which shows mainly small amplitude axonal spikes compared to the larger spikes in the neighboring gray matter
    c. ventricles, with no spikes and low amplitude voltage fluctuations
    d. CA1 layer of the hippocampus, with large amplitude spikes and a phase inversion of the local field potential. The latter part is not possible with NP1.0 data (those split the high-frequency ap band and the low frequency lfp band), but it is possible from 2nd Gen NP probes
